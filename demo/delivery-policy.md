## Delivery and returns

Shopping online should feel straightforward. Customers need to understand the delivery cost and their options for returning an order before they pay, rather than discovering an unexpected charge or searching for the policy afterwards. This explanation will appear on the checkout help page, where people want a quick answer without reading a long document.

Delivery is free on orders of $50 or more. Orders below $50 have a delivery charge, shown at checkout before payment. The checkout also lists the available shipping options so customers can review the choices for their order. Keep the minimum order amount clear wherever free delivery is mentioned: leaving out the condition would promise a different offer.

Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. Instructions for starting a return are included in the order confirmation email. Customers who cannot find that email or who need help with an order can contact the support team. The final explanation should be short and welcoming, while still giving people enough information to decide whether to buy.
