## Workspace cleanup

Over time, a workspace fills up with downloaded attachments, exported previews and temporary working files. People rarely go back through all of these items, so material they no longer need can make the workspace harder to navigate. We want automatic cleanup to reduce that clutter without making people worry about losing something they deliberately saved.

Automatically delete files that have not been opened in the past 30 days. Use the last-opened date to decide whether a file qualifies, rather than its creation date: an old file that someone still opens regularly is still useful. The cleanup should happen in the background so people do not have to review a long list of forgotten downloads.

Starred files must always be kept, even when they have not been opened for more than 30 days. A star means that the person wants to keep the file. That choice takes priority over the automatic cleanup rule; otherwise, a feature intended to tidy the workspace could remove precisely the material someone took care to save.
