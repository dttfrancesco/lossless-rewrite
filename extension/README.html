Keep ideas in the conversation.

  1. Select a passage in your chat. Lossless remembers your last selection when you move to the chat box. It keeps the meaning by default. Choose Keep meaning to save several passages, or Keep wording for exact text.
  2. In the chat box, write what you want the AI to do, such as Shorten this to 150 words. Keep wording saves a passage; it does not choose the editing instruction.
  3. Press Ctrl+Shift+Enter (Mac: Command+Shift+Enter) to send the chat draft with your remembered selection and marked passages. This also works while the source text is selected. Alt+Shift+L (Mac: Option+Shift+L) adds /lossless to your draft without sending. Then use normal Send. Without selections or enabled writing rules, Lossless leaves the draft unsent.

Lossless stays hidden during ordinary chat. Use the extension icon → Selections & PDF to review passages or open a reference. Close notices with × or Escape; send receipts disappear automatically.

Ordinary messages and Shift-Enter are unchanged. Lossless uses the current site's model. An unrecognized send control leaves the prepared text for manual sending. After sending, Lossless watches for the finished reply. Exact wording is checked locally; meaning checks require the setup below. Fix in chat prepares a repair for you to review and send. Word limits are requests to the model, not enforced guarantees.

Open Shortcuts in the sidebar header or ? in the inline popup for the keyboard guide.

Show kept text

After the reply finishes, press Alt+Shift+H or choose Show kept text. Press again to hide it. Amber marks matching wording from Keep wording selections; green marks matches from Keep meaning selections. This is a local text search that ignores layout whitespace, not a semantic check. Paraphrases are not located automatically. Unsupported reply layouts use the full editor.

Check meaning without an API key

  1. Open Account and choose Continue with Google.
  2. On Free, choose Check finished reply after sending with Lossless. Private test accounts check automatically when the chat signals that the reply is complete. Plus and Pro will offer this when they launch. If completion cannot be detected, use the check button.

Free includes 25 check credits each calendar month. Larger checks show their cost before running. Plus and Pro are coming soon. Their interest buttons collect your verified email and chosen plan for launch updates, with no payment or subscription. Use the same buttons to remove your interest, or use the unsubscribe link in your confirmation email. No sign-in is needed to unsubscribe by email.

Optional: use your own Jev key

Choose Set up checks → Use your own Jev key instead in the sidebar. Put TYPESAFE_API_KEY in the repository's .env.local file, not in a chat, then install the local companion. The companion guide has platform-specific instructions. Enable automatic companion checks in that setup dialog. Account checks take priority when you are signed in.

Highlights and quick controls

A small round Lossless button appears when the sidebar is closed. Drag it to move it. Click it for Source, Reply, Selections, PDF, Sidebar and Hide. The logo stays in the centre of the open menu. With no marked passages, Add source opens selection guidance. Source and Reply switch their highlights independently. Use × to hide the launcher; reopen it from Selections & PDF in the sidebar. Amber marks Keep wording, green Keep meaning, and blue other reply wording. Matching colours are not a semantic verdict; paraphrases may be blue. Check results stay behind the logo instead of opening a popup. Alt+Shift+H toggles both sets of highlights. Escape or clicking outside closes the controls.

Check a rewrite

Open Check a rewrite in the sidebar for the full editor, exact wording checks, chat-based meaning reviews, evidence, undo and repair prompts. For Jev, choose Jev · local connection in Sites and connections. The companion uses only the decision model: uncertain results stay Needs review, without a second-model call. Writing, optional idea extraction and repairs use prompts you send in your existing chat. Restart Chrome after updating the companion. These full-editor controls are separate from the inline checks in your chat.

My writing rules

Open Writing rules in the sidebar. Add one specific instruction per line, then choose Remember these rules. Saved rules apply to future Lossless sends in all chats on this device. You can edit, remove or turn them off. Supported explicit preferences at the start of sent messages can be remembered locally, with Undo. Turn capture off in My writing rules. Old conversations and AI replies are not scanned. Press Alt+Shift+R (Option+Shift+R on Mac) to open these settings. Use specific requirements rather than the name of a style guide.

Sign in to check each writing rule with Jev alongside your selected ideas. Results distinguish rules followed, not followed and needing review. Fix in chat includes failed rules in the repair request. Rules contribute to the quoted check size. They work even without marked passages. The full editor and local companion do not currently check these saved rules.

PDF beside your chat

Open PDF replaces the Lossless side panel with a reader, leaving your chat visible. Drop a PDF into it or click to choose a file. Select text and use Keep meaning or Keep wording, then write and send your request in the chat. Only the selected passages are included. If the AI needs the entire PDF, attach it in the chat as well. Lossless menu returns to the usual sidebar controls.

Your text

Chat selections use extension session storage and are scoped to a chat tab and conversation. Clear selections removes them; closing the chat tab clears its selections. PDF files stay on your device. Selected passages, filenames and page numbers are included when you send with the Lossless shortcut or /lossless. The chosen chat provider's terms and privacy settings apply.

The PDF reader supports selectable text and files up to 25 MB. Scanned pages need OCR elsewhere. Open downloaded references in this reader; Chrome's built-in PDF viewer is not modified.

Accounts and data

Read the privacy policy.

Google sign-in uses Supabase authentication. Hosted checks send your marked passages, enabled writing rules and the reply to our Supabase endpoint and then Jev. We store account, subscription and usage records, not document text in the application database. Provider processing policies also apply. Exact wording checks run locally. There is no analytics tracking.

PDF files stay on your device. Pro reference projects save PDFs and selections encrypted in your browser profile; the encryption key is also stored on that device. This does not protect against someone who can use your unlocked browser profile. The full editor's separate Save locally feature is not encrypted. Remove saved projects in the PDF reader and clear saved editor documents in the editor.

Checkout is disabled during the free launch. Plan interest is stored in Supabase. Joining sends a confirmation email through Brevo to your verified address. We retain delivery status to prevent duplicate confirmations and a hashed token for the email unsubscribe link. Brevo processes email delivery and link events. Private test access is enabled only for verified email addresses on a server-managed allowlist. Version 0.5.15 is available from source; Chrome Web Store publication is not yet confirmed. Users have reported working flows on all five supported platforms; layouts and account variants may differ.
