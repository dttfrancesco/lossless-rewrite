Keep ideas in the conversation.

  1. Open your chat. Lossless appears beside the composer on supported sites after Chrome grants access.
  2. Select a passage and choose Keep meaning or Keep wording. For references, choose Open PDF and select passages in the local reader.
  3. Type /lossless before your request and use the usual Enter key or Send button. Your selected passages are included automatically.

Ordinary messages and Shift-Enter are unchanged. Lossless uses the current site's model. An unrecognized send control leaves the prepared text for manual sending. Sending does not verify or repair the answer.

Check a rewrite

Open Check a rewrite in the sidebar for the full editor, exact wording checks, chat-based meaning reviews, evidence, undo and repair prompts. Automated API/CLI checks use the optional local companion described in companion/README.md.

Your text

Chat selections use extension session storage and are scoped to a chat tab and conversation. Clear selections removes them; closing the chat tab clears its selections. PDF files stay on your device. Selected passages, filenames and page numbers are included when you submit /lossless. The chosen chat provider's terms and privacy settings apply.

The PDF reader supports selectable text and files up to 25 MB. Scanned pages need OCR elsewhere. Open downloaded references in this reader; Chrome's built-in PDF viewer is not modified.

The extension has no analytics or hosted collection endpoint. Optional companion operations send necessary text to configured providers. There is no hosted subscription service. Version 0.4.0 is an unpacked preview; installed provider compatibility still requires validation.
