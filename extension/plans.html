How checks work

A check covers a completed reply and the ideas you marked, not one check per idea. Checking a revised reply uses another check. Reopening an unchanged result would not use another check.

Long documents may use more checks; their cost must be shown before running. Your existing chat handles writing and repairs using its own account allowance. Checks can be wrong.

The current preview is free and has no paid accounts or included check allowance. Jev checks currently need your own key and the local companion. The existing local PDF reader remains available; Pro would add persistent reference projects.
