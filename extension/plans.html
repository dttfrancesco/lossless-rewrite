How checks work

A check covers a completed reply and the ideas you marked, not one check per idea. Running another check uses credits; viewing an already displayed result does not.

Long documents may use more checks; their cost must be shown before running. Your existing chat handles writing and repairs using its own account allowance. Checks can be wrong.
